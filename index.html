About

BDCMUN: Remastered aims to foster fairness in the debating circuit through its randomised country allotment process, through which we will engender confidence in delegates irrespective of their institutional background. This revolutionary allotment process and our impartial judging establish BDCMUN as a hub of high-quality debate that circumvents institutional bias and cultivates knowledgeable, eloquent leaders.
